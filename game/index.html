SOLARA CITY

A sun-soaked open city. Walk its streets, steal a car, and make the city yours. Traffic fills the roads, pedestrians crowd the sidewalks, and the Solara PD is watching — steal a car or hit a pedestrian and they will come for you. Find Bang Dar at the park corner: he has work for you.

HOW TO PLAY

WASD / Arrows — walk & drive
Mouse — look around (click the game to lock the mouse)
Shift — run
E — enter / steal car, exit car, advance dialog
F or Click — punch
P — pause  ·  H — this help  ·  M — mute sound
